# Tennis / Sports Video Analytics: Market and Business Models (as of Nov 2026)

## SwingVision: pricing, users, funding, tech, partnerships, line calling

### Takeaway
SwingVision is the clear amateur-market leader. It is a consumer iPhone/iPad subscription app, with a roughly $6M Series A (Oct 2023) and about $8-10M raised in total according to trackers that disagree with each other. No public revenue or current user count was found. Pricing is about $120-360 a year, tiered by features.

### Cited Findings
- Series A of $6M in Oct 2023, led by Authentic Ventures. Participants included Lindsay Davenport, Alison Riske, Rohan Bopanna, Youssef Es-skouri (Dropbox) and Glenn Solomon (GGV). Stated purpose: expand into pickleball and eventually padel. — [SGI Europe](https://sgieurope.com/ai-powered-tennis-app-swingvision-raises-6-million/105552.article); [Profluence](https://profluence.com/swingvision-raises-6m-to-reach-pickleball-and-padel/)
- Earlier rounds are reported as a $600K seed (Apr 2020), a $2M seed (Jun 2021) and a $2M round (2022, with Wildcard Ventures and Tennis Australia per StartupIntros). — [Tennis Nerd](https://www.tennisnerd.net/press-releases/swingvision-raises-2-million-dollars/26870); [StartupIntros](https://startupintros.com/news/swingvision-seed-2022-01-31)
- Total raised conflicts across trackers: Dealroom about $10M over 4 rounds, CB Insights $8.12M over 4 rounds on one profile and $9.72M over 6 rounds on another. A March 2026 "Other Investors" round appears on CB Insights with no amount (UNVERIFIED). — [Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/companies/swingvision); [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/swingvision/financials); [CB Insights alt](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/swingvision-1/financials)
- Pricing (per search summaries of App Store listings, so ESTIMATE-grade and varying by region): Plus $119.99/yr or $14.99/mo; Pro $179.99/yr or $24.99/mo; Max $359.99/yr or $39.99/mo. A tracker checked Jul 2026 shows Pro at $179.99/yr and $24.99/mo but Plus yearly at $95.99, which conflicts with the $119.99 above. An older 2023 listing showed Pro at $199.99/yr; Australia showed $299.99/yr. The app is free to download, with a trial. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/swingvision-tennis-pickleball/id989461317); [AppPricingLab](https://apppricinglab.com/iap/apple/989461317)
- Users: the only figure found is about 10,000 monthly users, from Apple's 2022 profile, so it is stale (4+ years). No current user or download count found. — [Apple Newsroom 2022](https://apple.com/newsroom/2022/05/swupnil-sahai-and-his-co-founder-serve-an-ace-with-ai-powered-swingvision); [AppleInsider forum](https://forums.appleinsider.com/discussion/227609)
- Partnerships (CONFIRMED by LTA announcement): the LTA (UK) named SwingVision its Official Player and Ball Tracking Technology Partner under a three-year deal, and the same announcement says it is also Tennis Australia's official partner. Apple has featured the app multiple times. Tennis Australia also appears as an investor per StartupIntros. The App Store lists "USTA Connect" (shows verified NTRP/WTN ratings), which is a feature, not a documented partnership. No source found for ATP/WTA or Wimbledon partnerships. — [LTA](https://www.lta.org.uk/news/lta-continue-drive-for-innovation-in-tennis-with-new-swingvision-partnership/); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/swingvision-tennis-pickleball/id989461317)
- Line calling: the company claims 97% correct for balls landing within 10 cm of a line, vs about 90% for the human eye (company-sourced via Tennis.com). The CEO said in a podcast that a two-phone setup exceeded 99% and that certification was being pursued (self-reported). SwingVision ran live line calling at four USTA Southern California tournaments, but no error data was published. — [Tennis.com](https://www.tennis.com/news/articles/fair-play-swingvision-puts-its-electronic-line-calling-to-the-test-in-tournament-play); [Second Serve Tennis](https://www.buzzsprout.com/1045900/episodes/17866950-end-of-disputed-line-calls-swingvision-s-answer)
- An ITF approval report exists (PAT 25-037). Per the search summary it states that approval does not establish the accuracy or reliability of in/out decisions (not verified against the PDF). — [ITF](https://www.itftennis.com/media/15294/pat-25-037-approval-report-swingvision.pdf)
- A "Smart Court" product (SwingVision's club-oriented, fixed-camera system) and a 2-camera option were reported by Tennis Nerd. I could not read the details such as pricing. — [Tennis Nerd](https://www.tennisnerd.net/news/swingvision-smart-court-complete-ai-system-for-tennis/45328)
- Tech approach: on-device iPhone/iPad computer vision (Apple platform, Apple developer spotlights). Not verified beyond App Store/Apple coverage. — [Apple Developer Spotlight](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=nmvtcltj)

### Inferences
- If SwingVision had even 100k paying subscribers at about $120-180, revenue would be $12-18M ARR. This is an illustration only, since no subscriber count was found.
- Tennis Australia (investor) and the LTA (partner) show federations act as distribution channels, which a Europe-focused entrant would need to replicate (RFET, FFT, DTB).
- Expansion to pickleball and padel suggests tennis alone is seen as too small to carry a venture-scale outcome.

### Gaps
- No revenue, subscriber count or current download number. No primary confirmation of ATP/WTA/USTA partnerships. Smart Court pricing not obtained. Fetches of the primary pages failed.

## Competitors: PlaySight/Slinger, Hawk-Eye/Sony, Sportradar, Infosys/ATP, Baseline Vision, others

### Takeaway
Revenue pools differ sharply by segment. Sportradar (betting data) is by far the largest in the real numbers found. Pro-tour stats and officiating are B2B contracts with tours and tournaments. Club and amateur video is small and mostly venture-funded. Slinger/PlaySight and Baseline Vision are the most relevant club-level competitors, but I found no club price lists.

### Cited Findings
- Sportradar (public): FY2025 revenue up 17% to a record EUR 1,290M, profit EUR 100M, and it completed the IMG ARENA acquisition. Its tennis exposure is mainly betting data and integrity; "Foresight" covers up to 14,000 matches on the UTR pro tennis tour. The revenue is not tennis-specific and is driven by betting. — [Sportcal](https://www.sportcal.com/news/sportradar-hits-record-revenue-in-2025-financial-results/); [Finviz FY2025 release](https://finviz.com/news/327964/sportradar-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025-financial-results-and-announces-significant-expansion-in-share-repurchase-plan-to-1-billion)
- Slinger Bag acquired PlaySight Interactive for $82M (about Oct 2021 per Dealroom and the Gadgets & Wearables URL date; a Front Office Sports page shows a 2026 page date, so timing is ambiguous in the sources). No PlaySight revenue or pricing found. — [Front Office Sports](https://frontofficesports.com/slinger-acquires-playsight-interactive-for-82m); [Dealroom](https://dealroom.co/companies/playsight-interactive/); [Gadgets & Wearables](https://gadgetsandwearables.com/2021/10/13/slinger-bag-playsight/)
- Slinger Bag hardware: launcher about $649, Tournament Pack about $749 on Slinger's site (retailers $699-$999; UK about GBP 795). Slinger app: free plan with 2 hours of monthly analysis, Pro $9.99/month for 10 hours. Also reported (per snippet, unclear source quality) is a claim of about $250M revenue over five years, which is a forward-looking estimate. Trustpilot reviews mention price increases. — [Slinger](https://slingerbag.com/pages/tennis); [Tennis Warehouse](https://www.tennis-warehouse.com/Slinger_Tennis_Tournament_Pack_Ball_Machine/descpage-SBEAGSP.html?img=2); [Trustpilot](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/slingerbag.com)
- Infosys: ATP digital innovation partner since 2015 (Beyond the Numbers, scores centre, AI chatbot "Ally"). Contract value and term are not public. A reported 26%/80% uplift in social impressions/traffic is Infosys self-reporting. — [Infosys ATP](https://www.infosys.com/atp/tennis-platform.html); [ATP 2018 renewal](https://www.atptour.com/-/media/sites/atp-tour/press/press-releases/27-june-2018-atp-infosys-partnership-renewal.pdf)
- Baseline Vision (Israel): sold as a subscription to clubs, federations and academies with no install or upfront cost, per a company profile; another listing says from $1,999 one-time. The two conflict and no official price list was found. Funding about $3.7M per one tracker ($100K pre-seed Dec 2018, $2.4M seed Dec 2023), with an Israel Innovation Authority investment. It joined the In/Out line-calling ecosystem in Feb 2024. — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/baseline-vision/financials); [Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/baseline-vision); [Gadgets & Wearables](https://gadgetsandwearables.com/2024/02/10/baseline-vision-in-out/); [SourceForge](https://sourceforge.net/software/product/Baseline-Vision/)
- Low-end coach tools: Tennis Coach Plus app is $2.99 on the App Store, showing the price floor for manual video-review apps. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id432272928)

### Inferences
- The business-model split is: betting-data licensing (Sportradar, high margin, large), tour/federation platform contracts (Infosys), hardware plus subscription (Slinger), and consumer app subscription (SwingVision).
- Price anchors for clubs run from low $/month apps to a multi-thousand-dollar fixed install, with a gap in the middle for a coach-managed, per-seat product.

### Gaps
- Hawk-Eye/Sony revenue and Electronic Line Calling economics: no result found. Tennis Data Innovations: no result found. "Mojo": no tennis video product found under that name. PlaySight camera-system pricing, cloud tagging company pricing, and federation-level contract prices were not found. Infosys contract value not public.

## Market size estimates

### Takeaway
Generic sports-analytics market numbers range from about $4.5B to $9.6B for 2025 with CAGRs of 20-27%. They come from market-research vendors and should be treated as unreliable. No tennis-specific market figure was found.

### Cited Findings
- Fortune Business Insights: about $5.79B (2025), 20.5% CAGR 2026-2034; North America $2.38B (41.2%). Market Research Future: $4.55B (2025) rising to $42.68B (2035). Technavio: 27% CAGR 2025-2030, about $9.55B incremental opportunity. MarketsandMarkets: Europe about $704.8M (2025). — [Fortune Business Insights](https://fortunebusinessinsights.com/press-release/global-sports-analytics-market-10825); [MRFR](https://www.marketresearchfuture.com/reports/sports-analytics-market); [Technavio](https://technavio.com/report/sports-analytics-market-industry-analysis); [MarketsandMarkets Europe](https://www.marketsandmarkets.com/Market-Reports/geography/sports-analytics-market/Europe)
- Quality flag: these vendors define "sports analytics" differently (often including betting data and wearables) and the figures diverge by about 2x for the same year. The 2035 forecast is about 9x the 2025 base. The search also returned Fortune pages with mismatched URLs, which lowers confidence. Sportradar's actual EUR 1.29B revenue is a harder anchor for the data/betting part of the industry.

### Inferences
- Tennis amateur video analytics is likely a small niche (tens to low hundreds of $M at most), but that is my inference with no source behind it.

### Gaps
- No tennis-specific market size, no ITF/TIA player counts retrieved (only padel data below).

## Demand, willingness to pay, accuracy complaints

### Takeaway
Direct evidence on coach willingness to pay is absent. Accuracy evidence for SwingVision is mostly vendor-reported, with only anecdotal skeptical comments.

### Cited Findings
- No survey of tennis coaches' willingness to pay for analysis software was found. The closest evidence is a Swiss amateur football study finding the optimal price was 83.3% below the vendor's offered price. That is a club sample in a different sport. — [ZHAW thesis](https://srv-clst-301-data66.zhaw.ch/bitstream/11475/23304/3/2021_Huetterman_Video_Production_and_Distribution_Platform_in_Swiss_Sports_Teams.pdf)
- Review aggregator and podcast comments: one reviewer expected more accuracy, and a podcast host said some easy calls still trip the app. Anecdotal only. No Reddit r/10s threads were retrieved. — [JustUseApp reviews](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/989461317/swingvision-a-i-tennis-app/reviews); [Second Serve Tennis](https://www.buzzsprout.com/1045900/13802829-swingvision-magic-tennis-technology-part-3)
- Clubs are experimenting with low-cost coach-video services: an LTA club (Bramford) offers video review where players send clips to an accredited coach for a small fee. — [ClubSpark](https://clubspark.lta.org.uk/BramfordTennisClub/Coaching/VIDEOANALYSIS)

### Inferences
- Accuracy complaints concentrate on line calls near lines and on single-phone setups, which the two-camera/Smart Court products target. A coach tool that avoids line-calling claims and focuses on stroke/tactic tagging avoids that exposure.

### Gaps
- No Reddit/App Store review sampling, no coach survey data. Needs a direct pull of App Store reviews and r/10s threads.

## Opportunity for a Spain/Europe coach-oriented product

### Takeaway
Plausible but unproven. The data found points to padel outgrowing tennis in Spain, so a tennis-only product faces a shrinking core and should likely plan for padel. No Spain tennis licence total or Europe participation figure was retrieved.

### Cited Findings
- Spain had almost 4,500 padel clubs and 17,000 courts, and the padel federation issued 109,040 licences in 2024 (+8%), per FIP. — [FIP](https://www.padelfip.com/2025/02/padel-in-spain-growth-in-courts-and-player-licenses-in-2024-morcillo-these-results-reflect-the-hard-work-of-recent-years/)
- An academic study of federation licences 1994-2016 found padel up 85% (2001-2016) while tennis fell 27.1%, and predicted padel overtaking tennis. Old data showing trend only. — [UPM study](https://polired.upm.es/index.php/materiales_historia_deporte/article/download/4082/4138)
- RFET publishes 2025-26 licence rules and club fee schedules, which could be a channel for federation-level distribution (not read in detail). — [RFET licence rules](https://www.rfet.es/descargar/file-rfet-gobierno-reglamentos/38_Adjunto/rfet-reglamento-de-licencias-2025-2026-a-partir-de-octubre-2025.pdf); [RFET fees](https://www.rfet.es/descargar/file-clubes-cuotas/5_Adjunto/cuotas-rfet-2025-2026.pdf)
- SwingVision's own padel/pickleball expansion plan and its federation partnerships (LTA, Tennis Australia) are the closest precedent for a federation-led go-to-market (see first section).

### Inferences
- Gaps competitors appear to leave: Spanish-language, coach-centred workflow (multi-player rosters, review sharing with parents/players), GDPR-compliant handling of minors' video, and EU federation integrations. None of this was validated by evidence; it is a hypothesis for interviews.
- Because SwingVision is iOS-centric (per the App Store listing), Android-heavy Spanish markets may be underserved. UNVERIFIED, needs a market-share check.

### Gaps
- No RFET licence total, Spanish tennis club count, Europe tennis participation figure (Tennis Europe/ITF), Android share data, or Spanish/EU competitor list (e.g. local academies' tools). Recommend follow-up research on these.
